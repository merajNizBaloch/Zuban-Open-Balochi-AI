# Zubán Community Code of Conduct

Zubán exists to build open language technology with and for Balochi communities.

Contributors must:

- treat speakers of every Balochi variety with respect;
- avoid harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language;
- discuss linguistic disagreements with evidence and context;
- respect privacy, consent, copyright and dataset licenses;
- never fabricate sources, benchmarks or model results;
- disclose conflicts of interest where relevant.

Maintainers may remove content or participation that materially harms the project or its community.
